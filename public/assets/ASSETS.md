## Loading notes (read before using)

- **Kenney GLBs in `env/castle`, `env/town`, `env/graveyard`, `env/dungeon-mini`, and `pets/cube-pets` reference an external `Textures/colormap.png`** (sibling folder). Keep the `Textures/` folder next to them; GLTFLoader resolves it relative to the GLB URL. All other GLBs are self-contained (embedded textures or vertex colors).
- Kenney Nature Kit (`env/nature`) uses flat vertex-colored materials (no textures) - recolor freely.
- KayKit Medieval Hexagon buildings (`env/village-kaykit`) are sized to sit on a ~1-unit hex tile: scale up (~3-6x) to make them walk-in size relative to a kitten hero. Colors `_blue`/`_red` = roof/banner tint; un-suffixed files are neutral.
- Kenney/KayKit animated characters with `sk=0` use **node (TRS) animation**, not skinning - still play with `AnimationMixer`.
- KayKit characters (Adventurers/Skeletons) share one rig; weapons from `weapons/kaykit` / `weapons/skeleton` attach to bones `handslot.r` / `handslot.l` (verified present in Knight.glb and Skeleton_Warrior.glb).
- Quaternius Ultimate Monsters / Cute Monsters / Animals share clip-name conventions per family (see clip sets). Note Quaternius' own spelling `HitRecieve`/`RecieveHit`.
- HDRIs are Radiance `.hdr` 1k: load with `RGBELoader`, then `PMREMGenerator` for `scene.environment` (good for silver-armor reflections).

## Gaps / caveats

- **No CC0 armored-knight cat model was found** in the sources searched (Quaternius, Kenney, KayKit, poly.pizza, OpenGameArt) - Swordcat herself should be procedural (or a modded `cats/Cat_Blocky.glb` with KayKit sword/shield attached). `Cat_Blocky` (skinned, 8 clips) is the best rigged cat; `Cat_LowPoly` has only Idle/Walking.
- No dedicated crow/raven model: use `creatures/monsters-flying/Pigeon.glb` (animated) tinted black, or static `creatures/polypizza/Bird.glb`.
- Wolves: `creatures/animals/Wolf.glb`, `Husky.glb`, `Fox.glb` (clip set S9: Attack, Death, Gallop, Walk, Idle_HitReact1/2...).
- Goblins/orcs: `creatures/polypizza/Goblin.glb`, `creatures/monsters-big/Orc.glb`, `Orc_Skull.glb`, `creatures/polypizza/OrcEnemy.glb`, Kenney `env/dungeon-mini/character-orc.glb`.
- Boss candidates: `creatures/monsters-big/MushroomKing.glb`, `creatures/monsters-flying/Dragon_Evolved.glb`, `creatures/cute-monsters/YellowDragon.glb`, `creatures/skeletons/Skeleton_Mage.glb`, `creatures/monsters-big/Demon.glb`.
- Music has no dedicated 'cute' main-menu theme; `village_bards_tale` / `festival_minstrel_dance` fit best. Victory jingle is short (~5 s); Kenney `sfx/jingles/` adds 85 short stingers.
- Kenney Nature Kit desert/beach items (cactus, palm, canoe, bed, beach platform) were pruned. Hexagon tiles (`hex_*`) from the KayKit hex pack were not imported (hex-grid specific).
