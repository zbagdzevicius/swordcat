# Trend notes (retired)

This file used to hold the 2026-10-07 trend research behind the original concept. That research described a
third-party viral meme and a fan video series, and it linked to outside posts, sites and media. On 2026-10-13 the
game moved to the Token Tails IP (Pawsbury Knight), those elements were removed from the game, and the research was
taken out of the working tree. Where the concept came from, and what changed, is recorded in
[`docs/PROVENANCE.md`](../docs/PROVENANCE.md).

## The `[TREND Vn]` tags in code comments

Older comments in `src/` cite `[TREND V1]` … `[TREND V7]`. They now mean only these generic staging ideas, none of
which is copied from any one source:

| Tag | Generic staging idea |
|---|---|
| V1 | A short, seamless idle loop with a still camera and wind in the cape (the camera's `MEME_LOOP` preset id is a legacy name) |
| V2 | One small defender facing a crowd that comes out of the fog; a reverse shot from behind her |
| V3 | A cozy snack moment interrupted by something big; the camera tumbles into the grass |
| V4 | Flying on a friendly dragon |
| V5 | A pull-back that reveals something huge beside her (scale contrast) |
| V6 | Holding a gate alone until help comes; dawn over the clouds |
| V7 | A golden glow on the blade for a charged move |

No outside media, handles, links or token names are kept in this repo's working tree. Whether to rewrite the git
history as well is a founder decision (see `docs/PROVENANCE.md`).
